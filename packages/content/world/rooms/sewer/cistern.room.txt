; Sewer, far east: the siphon and the east cistern.
; Lines: the walkway (floor row 22) anyone can run; the middle line (row 15-16); the high line (row 8-12).
; Siphon (x 0-31): the walkway dives through a culvert; the siphon roof is the middle line (posts, an arch to
; slide under); a brick chimney and a hanging wall (x 17-24) make a tic-tac shaft over a high ledge (row 12).
; East cistern (x 32-63): a grate bridge out to the landing under the east manhole (x 44); pillars, a buttress
; and the great pipe for the bravest. A fall into the cistern is fatal.
